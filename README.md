# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview
An interactive **Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard** built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, and regional business trends. The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful insights to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity.
- Compare sales performance across regions and product categories.
- Identify profit contributions by customer segment and product category.
- Explore regional sales trends and patterns.
- Enable interactive analysis using Year and Country filters.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** 2.32M
- **Total Profit:** 385K
- **Total Quantity:** 8K


## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Quick overview of sales, profit, and quantity.
- **Country Filter:** Explore performance by country.
- **Year Slicer:** Filter the dashboard by year.
- **Sales Trend Analysis:** Visualize changes in sales over time.
- **Regional Sales Analysis:** Compare sales performance across regions.
- **Profit by Segment:** Understand the contribution of different customer segments.
- **Profit by Category:** Compare profitability across product categories.
- **Profit by Shipping Mode:** Analyze profit distribution by shipping method.
- **Category-Region Matrix:** Compare product category performance across geographic regions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query for data preparation
- DAX for measures and calculations, where applicable
- Data visualization and business intelligence

## 💡 Business Value
This dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance, helping stakeholders monitor key metrics, compare regional results, identify profitable product categories, and explore sales patterns through interactive visualizations.
